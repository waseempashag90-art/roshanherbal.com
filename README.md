# Roshan Herbal - Official Website

A fully functional, luxury e-commerce and brand website for **Roshan Herbal** (Nature's Power, Trusted Wellness).

## Features Included
1. **Royal Emerald & Gold Theme**: Designed to match the brand identity, logo, and product packaging.
2. **Product Catalog**:
   - **Product 1**: Hair & Relaxation Oil (100 ML - Rs. 549) with high-res bottle & full flyer label view.
   - **Product 2**: Body Massage Oil (100 ML - Rs. 399) with high-res bottle & full flyer label view.
   - **Product 3**: Shabab Bahar / شباب بہار (50g - Rs. 399) pure Himalayan Shilajit & royal vitality tonic with full benefits & ingredients.
3. **Interactive Shopping Cart**:
   - Add to cart with quantity controls.
   - Dynamic Pakistani shipping calculation (Free Delivery on orders above Rs. 1,800).
   - Cash on Delivery (COD) Checkout form with address validation.
   - Instant WhatsApp Checkout sending formatted order summary to `+92 336 3916555`.
4. **Direct WhatsApp Integration**:
   - 1-Click WhatsApp Order on every product card and detail modal.
   - Pulsing floating WhatsApp widget with instant quick responses.
5. **Product Modal**:
   - Switch between 3D Bottle View and Full Printed Label / Flyer view.
   - Detailed benefits, ingredients breakdown, Urdu instructions, cautions, and batch verification.
6. **Store Location & Contact**:
   - Phone / WhatsApp: `+92 336 3916555`
   - Store Address: `New City Phase 2, Wah Cantt, Pakistan`
   - Embedded Google Map view.
7. **Customer Reviews**:
   - Verified feedback with star ratings and a working submission form.

## How to Run
Simply double click `index.html` to open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox), or serve using Python:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
